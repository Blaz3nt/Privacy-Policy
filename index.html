SYNCLYTICS Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2025-11-08

SYNCLYTICS ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our app SYNCLYTICS ("Software"), including beta versions.

Important: SYNCLYTICS does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal information. All personal data (trainer names, operator names, employee IDs, equipment asset IDs, GPS coordinates) remains stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

1. Personal Information

SYNCLYTICS does NOT collect any personal information.

The following data remains stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Trainer names and employee IDs
  • Operator names and employee IDs
  • Equipment asset IDs or serial numbers
  • Session notes
  • GPS coordinates (if enabled in your device settings)
  • Company names or identifiers

This information is entirely under your control and can be exported or deleted at any time.

2. Operational Metrics We Collect

We collect ONLY anonymized operational metrics that cannot be linked to any individual, company, or location:

  • Equipment performance data: Equipment type (e.g., "Excavator", "Shovel"), bucket capacity (cubic meters), rated payload (tonnes)
  • Cycle performance data: Stage durations (dig, swing, dump times in milliseconds), cycle durations, bucket fill factor percentages, tonnes per hour calculations
  • Operating conditions: Material P80 fragmentation size (millimeters), material hardness ratings (soft, medium, hard, very hard)
  • Session metadata: Session duration, number of cycles per session, timestamp (date/time only, no location)

What We Do NOT Collect:

  • ❌ Names of trainers or operators
  • ❌ Employee IDs
  • ❌ Equipment asset IDs or serial numbers
  • ❌ Company names or identifiers
  • ❌ GPS coordinates or location data
  • ❌ Device identifiers (IMEI, serial numbers, MAC addresses)
  • ❌ IP addresses
  • ❌ Email addresses or phone numbers

3. How We Use Operational Metrics

Anonymized operational metrics are used for:

  • Industry benchmark development: Creating performance baselines for different equipment types and operating conditions
  • Statistical analysis: Understanding industry-wide efficiency trends
  • Software improvement: Identifying common usage patterns to enhance features
  • Research: Contributing to mining equipment efficiency research
  • Aggregate reporting: Publishing industry reports with aggregated data (no individual identification)

Example of aggregated data: "Excavators operating in hard material (P80 800mm) average 18.5 seconds per dig cycle" or "Shovels with 34m³ buckets achieve 92% average bucket fill factor."

These aggregated insights cannot be traced back to any specific operator, company, or location.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

Personal Information: We do not share personal information because we do not collect it.

Operational Metrics: Anonymized metrics may be shared with:

  • Third-party analytics providers (for statistical analysis only)
  • Cloud hosting providers (AWS, Google Cloud) for secure data storage
  • Research institutions (for academic studies on equipment efficiency)
  • Aggregated industry reports (individual users are not personally identified)

We do NOT:

  • Sell or rent any data to third parties
  • Share data that can identify individuals or companies
  • Provide raw session data to anyone outside SYNCLYTICS

Data may be stored on international servers (e.g., AWS US, Google Cloud EU) with industry-standard security measures.

5. Legal Basis & Consent

By using SYNCLYTICS and enabling automatic uploads, you consent to the collection and use of anonymized operational metrics as described in this Privacy Policy.

No personal information is collected, so no consent for personal data processing is required.

You may withdraw consent by:

  • Disabling automatic uploads in app settings (benchmarking features will be limited)
  • Uninstalling the app (deletes all local data from your device)
  • Contacting us to request deletion of operational metrics from our servers

If you withdraw consent, previously collected anonymized metrics may remain on our servers for up to 5 years for benchmarking purposes.

6. Your Rights (Australia)

  • Access: Request access to any information we hold (though we do not hold personal information)
  • Correction: Correct inaccurate information (applicable only to operational metrics)
  • Deletion: Request deletion of operational metrics from our servers
  • Export: Export all your local data in JSON or CSV format at any time
  • Complaints: Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@synclytics.tech.

7. Security

  • Local storage: Data on your device is encrypted using SQLite encryption
  • Transmission: Data transmitted to our servers is secured using HTTPS (TLS 1.3)
  • Server storage: Data is stored on secure cloud servers (AWS/Google Cloud) with encryption at rest
  • Access control: Access to servers is restricted and audited

While we implement reasonable security measures, no system can guarantee 100% security. We take no responsibility for unauthorized access due to factors beyond our control.

8. Data Retention

  • On your device: Data remains indefinitely until you delete it or uninstall the app
  • On our servers: Anonymized operational metrics are retained for up to 5 years for benchmarking purposes
  • After retention period: Metrics are archived or permanently deleted

9. Children's Privacy

The Software is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us immediately at support@synclytics.tech.

10. Third-Party Services

SYNCLYTICS integrates with the following third-party services:

  • AWS / Google Cloud: Cloud hosting for anonymized operational metrics
  • Resend: Email delivery for user feedback submissions

We do NOT use:

  • Advertising networks
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Behavioral analytics (e.g., Mixpanel, Amplitude)
  • User profiling or targeting services

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect:

  • Changes in legal requirements
  • New features or data collection practices
  • User feedback and improvements

Updated versions will have a new effective date at the top of this document. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

Material changes: If we make material changes (e.g., start collecting personal information), we will provide prominent in-app notice and request explicit consent before implementing changes.
